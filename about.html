About SnapDown

SnapDown is a modern web utility designed to simplify downloading public video and audio content from major platforms seamlessly, quickly, and securely.

Lightning Fast
Fetch and download media links in seconds without waiting in queues or dealing with tedious popups.
Zero Account Needed
We prioritize your privacy. No registration, no passwords, and no personal data tracking required.
Multiple Resolutions
Choose your preferred resolution from crisp 1080p Full HD down to compact 360p or standalone MP3 audio formats.
Cross-Platform Support
Works smoothly across all modern web browsers on desktop PCs, MacBooks, iPhones, iPads, and Android smartphones.

Our Mission

Our mission at SnapDown is to make content accessible for offline viewing and personal archival purposes. Millions of creators and users upload valuable educational, entertaining, and creative content online every day. We believe users should have a straightforward, friction-free tool to save public media for offline study, travel, or archiving without hassle.

Privacy & Responsibility

SnapDown operates strictly as a link retrieval tool. We do not host, store, or re-upload media files onto our servers. All stream links are parsed on demand. We respect content owners' rights and encourage all users to download content only in compliance with local copyright regulations and platform fair-use guidelines.

For more information regarding how we handle technical data, check out our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
